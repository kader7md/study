車検 Reception Script

How the shaken (vehicle inspection) works, what every fee on the estimate means, and the Japanese you say to the customer, written in romaji. Based on the consent form and the estimate email from your team.

What shaken is

Shaken (車検, shaken) is Japan's required vehicle inspection. Every car on the road must pass it. For a private passenger car it is every 2 years (after the first 3 years for a new car). Without a valid shaken the car cannot legally be driven, and the compulsory insurance (自賠責) must be renewed at the same time.

Officially it is called 継続検査 (keizoku kensa), "continuing inspection". The government office is the 陸運局 (rikuunkyoku), the Land Transport Bureau.

The process, step by step

  1. Reception (受付, uketsuke)Customer brings the car. You collect the documents, explain the estimate, and get the consent form signed. If they need a loaner car (代車, daisha), they sign the loan form (貸出書, kashidashi-sho).
  2. Documents (必要書類, hitsuyou shorui)車検証 (shakenshou, vehicle registration certificate) + 自賠責保険証書 (jibaiseki hoken shousho, compulsory insurance certificate) + the signed parking-fine consent form.
  3. Check for unpaid parking finesThe shop asks the police (through the 自動車整備振興会, the car maintenance association) whether the car has an unpaid parking fine. If it does, the shaken cannot be finished until the customer pays it.
  4. 2-year legal inspection + maintenance (法定2年点検)Technicians inspect the car, steam-clean the engine room and underbody, check the safety items (headlight aim, exhaust gas), and reset the BMW service indicators (CBS).
  5. Extra repairs: call the customer firstIf parts need changing (brake pads, tires, wipers, oil leaks…), you call the customer, explain the cost, and get approval before any work. These costs are NOT in the base estimate.
  6. Paperwork at the Transport BureauThe shop submits the inspection result and pays the government fees (weight tax, stamp fee, compulsory insurance). Much of this is now sent electronically, which is why the customer signs the electronic-application consent.
  7. Hand-over (お引き渡し, o-hikiwatashi)Customer pays and gets the car back with the new 車検証, the new inspection sticker (検査標章, kensa hyoushou) for the windshield, and the new 自賠責 certificate. Tell them the next shaken is in 2 years.

The consent form, explained

車検を受けられるお客様へ (shaken wo ukerareru okyakusama e): "To customers getting a shaken"

1. 放置違反金滞納情報照会 (houchi ihankin tainou jouhou shoukai): checking unpaid parking fines

  • Since June 2006 (平成18年), if the police ordered the car's user to pay a parking fine (放置違反金, houchi ihankin) and it is still unpaid, the car cannot finish its next shaken.
  • So the shop asks the police (through the car maintenance association) whether there is an unpaid fine. The customer must sign to agree to this.
  • If the customer has an unpaid fine, they must pay it and bring the receipt. If they lost the payment slip, they contact the traffic department of the prefectural police to get a new one.

2. 電磁的方法による申請 (denjiteki houhou ni yoru shinsei): electronic application

  • Since April 2017 (平成29年), the shaken documents can be made and submitted electronically. The law requires the customer's consent first.
  • The checked boxes mean the customer agrees that:
    • the dealer applies for the shaken on the customer's behalf (申請の委任),
    • the safety-standards certificate (保安基準適合証) information is sent electronically,
    • the compulsory insurance (自賠責) certificate information is sent electronically.
  • The data is used only for the shaken.

What the customer fills in

  • ご記入日 (go-kinyuu-bi): today's date (年 year, 月 month, 日 day).
  • 使用者の氏名（社名）(shiyousha no shimei): name of the car's user, or company name.
  • ㊞: hanko stamp, or a handwritten signature (記名・押印または自署).
  • 登録番号 (license plate number) and 車台番号 (chassis number): usually the dealer fills these in from the 車検証.
  • The bottom part (照会書, 自動車整備事業者用) is for the dealer. The customer does not fill it.
