# Reference notes: ideas from games we study

Ideas only. Never copy files, models, textures or code from these games into this repo (it is public).

## RV There Yet (from its asset index, names only)
- **Map structure:** one long ride map made of biome sections (canyon/forest base, jungle, snow), plus a separate
  bonus mini-game level. It has checkpoints along the way, out-of-bounds zones, and a vehicle that follows splines in places.
- **Obstacles opened by cranks:** crank wheels that drive boom gates, bridges, lifts, gondolas and jump platforms.
  There are also light puzzles, pressure plates, turbines with switch cables, dynamite with a detonator for rock walls,
  rope bridges, ladders, caves with lifts, and ski lifts.
- **Terrain hazards:** quicksand, sinkholes, icy and slippery patches, breakable ice and ice floes, lava, radioactive
  zones, thorn bushes, geysers, waterfalls and landmines.
- **Scripted events:** falling boulders and icicles, a log blocking the road, rolling-boulder and trip-wire traps,
  storms with weather and time-of-day changes, a monster chase, and helicopter or missile set pieces.
- **Wildlife per biome:** bears, eagles, goats, crabs, monkeys, snakes, a gorilla, snow leopards, sharks, piranhas.
- **Items:** big plank, bridging ladder, ramp, vehicle flipper, jack, snow chains, a winch (pole, hook and remote),
  nail gun, power drill and screw holes, a welder at stations, motor oil, wheels, parts, a flare gun,
  fire extinguisher, machete (chops coconuts), food and drink, epipen, antidote, bear spray, revive station,
  shared-resource pickups, loot spawners, cosmetic pickups and collectible tapes.
- **HUD (from screenshots):** the vehicle has 2 bars at the top centre (body, plus a striped mechanics bar). A winch
  line sits above them. Small resource counters sit left of the bars. Player health and status are bottom left.
  Context key hints are on the right.

## Peak
- One huge vertical climbing level split into biome bands, with campfire checkpoints, stamina-based climbing,
  teammates helping each other up, and items that make climbing easier (ropes, pitons, food).
